# EasyMedical Features Guide

## Home Screen (Dashboard)

Your personalized medical hub showing your most important health information.

### What You'll See

**📊 Active Medications**
- Lists all unique medications extracted from your records
- Shows medications from doctors, pharmacies, and lab records
- Tap any medication to see which records mention it

**⚠️ Allergies**
- Displays all allergies in red warning badges
- Helps you remember important medication allergies
- Shared across all your records for safety

**📋 Recent Records**
- Shows your 3 most recent uploads (last 90 days)
- Quick view of provider, specialty, and date
- Tap to view full record details

**➕ Upload New Record**
- Large blue button to quickly add more records
- Takes you to the Upload screen

## Upload Screen

Three ways to add medical records to your hub.

### 📷 Camera (Photo Capture)
1. Tap the **Camera** tab
2. Press **Open Camera**
3. Take a photo of your medical document
4. Claude will automatically extract key information

**Best for:**
- Lab results on paper
- Prescription documents
- Hospital discharge summaries
- Doctor's notes

**Tips:**
- Use good lighting for clear photos
- Keep document flat and in frame
- Avoid shadows and glare
- One document per photo

### 🖼️ Gallery (Photo Library)
1. Tap the **Gallery** tab
2. Press **Open Gallery**
3. Select a photo from your device
4. Claude will process the image

**Best for:**
- Previously saved medical documents
- Screenshots of digital records
- Photos already on your phone
- Batch importing multiple records

**Tips:**
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG
- Larger images work better
- Multiple pages? Upload separately

### ✏️ Text (Manual Entry)
1. Tap the **Text** tab
2. Type or paste medical information
3. Press **Process & Upload**
4. Claude summarizes and organizes it

**Best for:**
- Information from patient portals
- Doctor visit notes
- Test result summaries
- Prescription lists
- Personal medical history

**Tips:**
- Include as much detail as possible
- Dates help Claude understand context
- Spelling out abbreviations helps
- Minimum 10 characters required

## Upload Processing

After you upload, Claude AI processes your record:

1. **Extracts Key Information**
   - Medical specialty (Cardiology, Lab, Pharmacy, etc.)
   - Provider or facility name
   - Medications mentioned
   - Known allergies
   - Conditions or diagnoses
   - Test results (if any)
   - Important dates

2. **Creates Plain-Language Summary**
   - Medical jargon translated to simple language
   - Anyone can understand what the document says
   - Perfect for discussing with family or other doctors

3. **Saves to Your Device**
   - Records stored securely on your phone
   - No cloud upload (optional in future)
   - You own all your data

4. **Shows Confirmation**
   - Success message with two options
   - View the processed record immediately
   - Return to home screen

## Specialties Screen (🏥)

### How It Works

1. **Grouped by Medical Specialty**
   - All Cardiology records together
   - All Orthopedics records together

2. **Provider Information**
   - Shows hospital or doctor name
   - Date of each record
   - Record count per specialty

3. **Expandable Sections**

   - Collapse to clean up view

## Record Details Screen

View complete information about any single record.

### What's Displayed

**Header Info**
- Provider/hospital name
- Medical specialty
- Upload date

**Original Document** (if image)
- Full photo of uploaded document
- Scroll to see entire image

**Summary** (AI-Generated)
- Plain language explanation
- Written for non-medical readers
- Highlights most important info

**Extracted Data**
- Medications (if found)
- Allergies (clearly marked with ⚠️)
- Conditions/diagnoses
- Test results with values
- Important dates

**Original Text** (if text input)
- Your original text entry
- Shows what was submitted

### Actions

**← Back**
- Returns to previous screen
- Preserves your location

**Delete**
- Permanently removes this record
- Confirmation required
- Cannot be undone

## Tips for Best Results

✅ **DO:**
- Use clear, well-lit photos

- Note test result dates

❌ **DON'T:**
